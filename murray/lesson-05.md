Title: "Ask, and it shall be given you," or, The Certainty of the Answer to Prayer (Matthew 7:7-8)

Next to the Father's love, the most important lesson in prayer is this: everyone who asks receives. Jesus repeats the promise six ways to lodge it deep in our hearts.

**Ask, seek, knock.** Asking may receive the gift. Seeking finds the Giver himself. Knocking gains entrance to abiding fellowship in the Father's home. None of them is in vain.

**Take the words as given.** Jesus knew our natural doubt and our tendency to treat prayer as a religious exercise with no expected answer. Don't weaken his promise with human reasoning or settle every difficulty first. Believe it, and he will explain it.

**Prayer has two halves.** The asking is ours and the giving is God's, and they belong together. When no answer comes, we shouldn't slip into a lazy "resignation." We should let the Spirit search us until we learn to pray in faith.

**Answered prayer should be normal.** Too many Christians are content without definite answers. The Father wants daily requests and daily answers.

**Even "no" is an answer.** Moses and Jesus in Gethsemane were each given a clear answer. We either withdraw a request that is not God's will or persevere until the answer comes.
