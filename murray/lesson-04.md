Title: "After this manner pray," or, The Model Prayer (Matthew 6:9)

Jesus gave us the very words to bring to the Father: a prayer simple enough for a child and rich enough to hold all God can give.

**"Our Father which art in heaven."** No Old Testament saint dared call God Father. This word holds the mysteries of redemption, new birth and faith. Knowing God's Fatherliness is both the first and the last lesson in prayer.

**God's interests come first.** Jesus reverses our usual order: Thy name, Thy kingdom, Thy will, and only then give us, forgive us, lead us. Forgetting ourselves in seeking God's glory brings the richer blessing. Christ's school especially trains intercessors who live for the Father's business.

**The three "Thy" petitions.** The Father's name is hallowed as God reveals its holiness in us by his Spirit. His kingdom's coming waits on our prayers. His will is to be done on earth with heaven's glad obedience, not merely endured.

**The three "us" petitions.** Bread, forgiveness and deliverance cover all personal need. Those who give themselves to God's interests can trust him for daily bread. Forgiveness must be real and heartfelt, and it requires a forgiving spirit toward others.

The heart that begins with "Thine" gains the faith to say "our."
