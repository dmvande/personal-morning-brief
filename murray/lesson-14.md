Title: "When ye stand praying, forgive," or, Prayer and Love (Mark 11:25)

Right after his great promise about believing prayer, Jesus adds a condition: forgive. "Have faith in God" sets our relationship with God right. "Forgive" sets our relationships with people right. Prayer from a heart wrong on either side cannot prevail.

**A recurring teaching.** Leave your gift at the altar and be reconciled first; "forgive us … as we forgive"; the unmerciful servant. Jesus seems to have seen that failing to love was the great sin of praying people and a main cause of weak prayer.

**Forgive as God forgives.** Every answered prayer rests on God's pardoning love. When that love fills the heart, it flows out to others. We resist wounded pride, defending our rights and excusing sharp words. Our forgiving love is the evidence of God's forgiving love in us.

**Daily life is the test of prayer.** Life is a whole. God judges our prayer not by the mood we work up while praying but by who we are when not praying. Unloving thoughts and words left unchecked hinder prayer.

**Love is the soil of faith.** Love for others is the evidence of love for the Father and gives confidence before him. In serving others, see Jesus in even the unlovely person and carry them in real love.

This is not perfect love but a fixed purpose to love as God loves. Suffering, forbearing love prevails with God.
