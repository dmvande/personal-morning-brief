Title: "Pray the Lord of the harvest," or, Prayer Provides Labourers (Matthew 9:37-38)

Jesus seldom told his disciples what to pray, but here he gave a specific request: ask the Lord of the harvest to send out labourers.

**Prayer is a real power.** Why should disciples pray for something God already sees? Because Jesus truly believed their prayer was needed and would help. As with Abraham, Moses and Samuel, God hands his work to his people and gives them authority to call on heaven's help. The supply of workers really does depend on our prayer.

**Two reasons we neglect it.** We lack Jesus' compassion for crowds "as sheep having no shepherd." We also lack faith that prayer brings definite results. Living close to Christ lets his compassion flow into us and his Spirit assure us that our prayer counts.

**Two blessings to ask for.** First, more men and women wholly given to God's service as ministers, missionaries and teachers. Christ, exalted, gives Spirit-filled people as his chief gifts. Second, that every believer would serve, with none left idle in the vineyard. Wherever helpers are lacking, in a Sunday school or in rescue work, prayer has a promise of supply.

**Set apart time for this.** It draws us into Christ's compassionate heart, shows us our royal place as God's fellow-workers, and lets us share in the joy of answered prayer.
