Title: "Whose is this image?" or, Prayer in Harmony with the Destiny of Man (Matthew 22:20; Genesis 1:26)

The image on a coin settled who it belonged to. In the same way, the image we bear decides our destiny: made in God's image, we belong to God, and prayer is what we were created for.

**Man's original calling.** God made humanity to fill, subdue and rule the earth as his representative. Like a trusted viceroy whose advice his king acts on, man was to bring down heaven's blessing on earth through his requests. The world's destiny was placed in man's will and prayer.

**Image made rule possible.** God could entrust such authority to man because of an inner likeness to himself. Man was fitted to be prophet, priest and king between God and creation. Even after the fall, prayer remains the proof of our likeness to God and the power that moves the hand that holds the universe.

**Abraham shows it restored.** He is never recorded praying for himself. His prayers for Sodom, Lot, Abimelech and Ishmael shaped the lives of those around him.

**Christ brings it back fully.** "Ask what ye will" is given to those who abide in Christ and live wholly for the Father's kingdom, not chiefly to those seeking blessings for themselves. Such believers act as priests and kings.

The church's calling is higher than it knows. Redeemed people are meant to help determine the earth's history through prayer.
