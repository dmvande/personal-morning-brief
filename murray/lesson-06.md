Title: "How much more?" or, The Infinite Fatherliness of God (Matthew 7:9-11)

Jesus grounds the certainty of answered prayer in something everyone knows: a father gladly hears his child. If sinful earthly fathers give good gifts, how much more will the Father in heaven?

**Live as a child, and you will pray as a child.** A child's request has power because of the relationship it comes from. A child who avoids the father's house and company cannot expect to receive whatever it asks. Childlike asking is inseparable from childlike living under the Spirit's leading.

**The promises sit inside the life.** In the Sermon on the Mount, the prayer promises are woven into the precepts. They are for children who are poor in spirit, who let their light shine, love their enemies, forgive, trust the Father for earthly needs and do his will.

**Not perfection, but a whole heart.** God does not demand flawless obedience. He asks for an honest, whole-hearted surrender to live as his child. Nothing more, and nothing less.

**Fill the heart with the Father's love.** Picture the best earthly father's tenderness, then multiply it beyond reckoning. Dwell in that love always, not only when we want something.

The first lesson is also the highest: to say well, "Abba, Father." Whoever can say this holds the key to all prayer.
