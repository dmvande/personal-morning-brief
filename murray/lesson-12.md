Title: "Have faith in God," or, The Secret of Believing Prayer (Mark 11:22-24)

How can we reach the faith that knows it receives whatever it asks? Jesus answered before giving the promise: "Have faith in God." Trusting a promise depends entirely on trusting the one who makes it.

**Faith is eye and ear before it is hand.** We often picture faith as the hand that takes. But first it is the eye that gazes on the living God and the ear that hears his voice. I must see the promiser's face and hear his voice before I have the courage to take what he offers. As we look to God and give him time, his presence and power stream into the soul. Through the Spirit, God's words become living words, not just words in a book. Our power to receive depends on our power to perceive.

**The prayer of faith grows from the life of faith.** Faith is surrender: yielding to the impression God makes on us. It is also fellowship, being bound to the friend who promises. For someone who knows the Father well and lives close to him, believing his promise is simple and natural. Such faith is God's gift, not poured in all at once but formed as a habit of soul through time spent with him.

**Look at God, not just the promise.** Many fix all their effort on gripping a promise, fail, and give up. A doctor asked to strengthen someone's grip would build up the whole body. So the cure for weak faith is a stronger spiritual life through fellowship with God.

**The saints show the pattern.** Abraham believed because God first revealed himself: "I am thy shield." Later, "I am God Almighty," and Abraham fell on his face while God talked with him. A promise has power only when God himself speaks it to us, and he speaks to those who walk with him.

We seek God's gifts, but he wants to give us himself first. Take time to bow before him and let him reveal himself. A heart full of God has power for the prayer of faith.
