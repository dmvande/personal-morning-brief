Title: "Have faith in God," or, The Secret of Believing Prayer (Mark 11:22-24)

How can we reach the faith that believes it receives whatever it asks? Jesus' answer comes before the promise: "Have faith in God." Trusting a promise depends entirely on trusting the one who makes it.

**Faith is eye and ear before it is hand.** We often picture faith as the hand that takes. But first it is the eye that gazes on the living God and the ear that hears his voice through the Spirit. Our power to receive depends on our power to perceive.

**The prayer of faith grows from the life of faith.** Faith is surrender and fellowship. For someone who knows the Father well and lives close to him, believing his promise becomes simple and natural. Such faith is God's gift, a habit of soul formed by time spent with him.

**Look at God, not just the promise.** Many fix all their effort on gripping a promise and give up when they can't. The cure for weak faith is to strengthen the whole spiritual life through fellowship with God, as a doctor builds up the whole body rather than the arm alone.

**The saints show the pattern.** Abraham believed because God revealed himself first: "I am thy shield," "I am God Almighty."

We seek God's gifts, but he wants to give us himself first. Take time to bow before him and let him reveal himself. A heart full of God has power for the prayer of faith.
