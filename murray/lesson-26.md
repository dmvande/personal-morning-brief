Title: "I have prayed for thee," or, Christ the Intercessor (Luke 22:32; John 16:26; Hebrews 7:25)

Spiritual growth comes from seeing more clearly what Jesus is to us. Prayer, too, need not be anxious effort. It is a sharing in Christ's own life of prayer.

**His intercession is real and essential.** Christ's work as priest on earth was only the beginning. Like Melchizedek, he now lives beyond the veil to intercede. Every conversion and every victory over sin flows from his asking and receiving. His mediation on the throne is as real and necessary as his work on the cross.

**We share in the work itself.** We are his body, and the head does not work without the members. His life in us is a praying life. These are not two separate streams of prayer: what he prays passes through us, and what we pray passes through him. He is the angel adding incense to the prayers of the saints.

**Christ is surety for our prayer life.** Many found a new beginning when they saw Christ as surety for their faithfulness. It is just as freeing to see him as the source of our praying. "I have prayed for thee, that thy faith fail not." His prayer doesn't replace our faith. It is the root of it.

**For others, not only ourselves.** These promises open to those who, like Jesus, live for perishing people. Find the souls entrusted to your prayers and make intercession for them your fellowship with God.
