Title: "Speedily, though bearing long," or, The Power of Persevering Prayer (Luke 18:1-8)

Why must a loving God be asked again and again, sometimes for years? When answers are delayed, it is tempting to stop praying and call it submission. Faith overcomes this temptation.

**Prayer accumulates.** Water must build up before it flows with full force. A farmer takes ten thousand steps and sows ten thousand seeds before the harvest. In the same way, no believing prayer is lost. Each is stored up and works toward the answer.

**Two words describe God.**
- *He will answer speedily.* The blessing is ready, and his love longs to give it. He won't delay a moment longer than necessary.
- *He is long-suffering.* Like a farmer waiting for the early and latter rain, the Father knows when a soul or church is ripe to receive and keep the blessing.

**Patience and faith together.** Faith holds the answer as already given in the promise and praises God for it. Patience keeps crying day and night until the answer is seen. There may also be hindrances in us or in the unseen world that persistent prayer must overcome.

**Give God time.** Don't let delay suggest the request isn't his will, if it rests on his Word and the Spirit's leading. Persevering prayer searches the heart, removes hindrances and draws us closer to Christ.

Pray this way especially for the church, which is like the helpless widow awaiting her Lord.
