Title: "In my Name," or, The All-Prevailing Plea (John 14:13-14; 15:16; 16:23-26)

In his farewell words Jesus repeats "in my name" again and again, attached to unlimited promises. His name is our only plea, and it is enough.

**What a name means.** A name stands for the whole person: his character, honour and power. To act in someone's name is to come with his authority as his representative. It always assumes shared interests and deep trust.

**Three pictures of the union behind the name.**
- *A business union.* A merchant gives a devoted chief clerk power to draw funds in his name for the firm's interests. Christ entrusted his kingdom's work to his servants, and they can use his name to the degree they live wholly for his interests.
- *A life union.* A child bears his father's name because he shares his life. Our power in Jesus' name matches our spiritual union with him.
- *A love union.* A bride takes her husband's name and buys freely in it. Bearing another's name means giving up our own independent life and gaining all that the name holds.

**The name rules the life.** "Do all in the name" and "ask all in the name" go together. God looks at our life, not our lips, to see what the name means to us. When it is everything to us, it will obtain everything for us.

**The promise guards itself.** No one can use the name beyond what they actually live.

Use Jesus' name to open heaven's treasures for a perishing world.
