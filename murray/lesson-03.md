Title: "Pray to thy Father, which is in secret," or, Alone with God (Matthew 6:6)

Jesus' first lesson in the Sermon on the Mount is that every disciple needs a secret place of prayer. Worship is not confined to places, yet each of us needs a fixed spot and a quiet time where Jesus can meet and teach us. That inner chamber is his schoolroom.

**Meet your Father there.** Jesus uses the name "Father" three times. Its atmosphere is God's Father-love, and the first thought in private prayer must be that the Father is waiting for me there.

**Come as you are.** When the heart feels cold, weak and prayerless, that is the very reason to go in. Think less about how little you bring and more about how much he wants to give.

**Secret prayer is never wasted.** "Thy Father … shall recompense thee." The blessing rests not on the strength of our feelings but on the Father's love and power, and it will show openly in our lives.

**Your Father knows what you need.** We need not pile up words to persuade an unwilling God. This truth brings a quiet, confident trust: "My Father sees, hears and knows."

Dwell much behind the closed door, alone with the Father.
