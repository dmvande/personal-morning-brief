Title: "In spirit and truth," or, The True Worshippers (John 4:23-24)

Jesus' first recorded teaching on prayer, given to the woman of Samaria, shows that the Father is actively seeking worshippers, and that he is seeking true ones.

**Three kinds of worship.** Jesus contrasts the Samaritans' ignorant worship and the Jews' informed worship with a new worship "in spirit and truth." This means more than sincerity or earnestness. Many Christians still pray earnestly without entering the third kind, which comes only through Christ.

**As God is, so his worshipper.** Because God is Spirit, our worship must match his nature. It cannot be confined to a place, an hour or a form. Prayer should be the spirit of our whole life, not a Sunday or closet exercise at odds with the rest of the week.

**The Spirit comes from God.** Only God can give Spirit. Christ, glorified, sent the Spirit of Sonship, by whom we cry "Abba, Father." True worship is worship of the Father by children.

**"In truth" means reality.** In Christ the shadows of the Old Testament become living fellowship with God.

**Our posture.** Confess our inability, wait teachably, and yield to the Spirit's breathing. Knowing God's infinite Fatherliness is the secret of prayer in spirit and truth.
