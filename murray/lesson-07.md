Title: "How much more the Holy Spirit," or, The All-Comprehensive Gift (Luke 11:13)

In Luke, Jesus repeats his "how much more" but names the gift: the Holy Spirit. This is the chief of the Father's gifts, the one that contains all the others, and the one he most delights to give.

**The highest gift a father can give.** A good father wants to reproduce his own spirit in his child. God can give nothing higher than his own Spirit. The Spirit sheds abroad the Father's love and breathes in us the Son's childlike devotion and obedience.

**The first object of prayer.** All fullness is in Jesus, and the Spirit makes it ours in experience. He is the Spirit of grace, faith, adoption, truth, prayer, holiness, power and glory, and he meets every need.

**Ask for more.** Believers already have the Spirit, yet like a branch thirsting for more sap, we keep asking for his fuller possession and guidance. God's promise to pour out his Spirit and his command to be filled set the measure of our expectation.

**Receive by faith, not feelings.** As we pray, we take the gift in faith and hold it with thanksgiving until it fills our whole being.

Those who learn this for themselves can pray with confidence for the Spirit to be poured out on others and on the whole church.
