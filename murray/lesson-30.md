Title: "An holy priesthood," or, The Ministry of Intercession (1 Peter 2:5; Isaiah 61:6)

Every redeemed person shares Christ's priestly anointing, as the oil on Aaron's head ran down to the hem of his robe. Every son of Aaron had a right to the priesthood, yet many believers never use theirs. It is our highest privilege and our closest likeness to Jesus, who ever lives to pray.

**The priest's work.** It faces two ways. Toward God, the priest draws near, dwells in his house, and offers blood and incense. Toward people, he receives their sacrifices, carries their sins and needs before God, and comes out to bless them in his name. A priest does not live for himself. Every believer is redeemed to be God's priest among the perishing millions, a steward of his grace.

**The priest's walk.** Aaron's sons wore holy garments made to God's pattern, kept free from defilement and death, had to be without blemish, and received no land, because God was their inheritance. Our power with God likewise depends on our life: unstained garments, separation from the world even in some lawful things, wholehearted consecration, and God alone as our portion.

**The priest's consecration.** Aaron's sons were priests by birth, yet they still had to be washed, clothed, anointed with oil, touched with blood on ear, hand and foot, and sprinkled again with blood and oil. A penitent's blood was sprinkled only on the altar. A priest's was applied to his own body. Believers are priests by new birth, but they exercise the office by entering fully into the blood and the Spirit. The blood cleanses the conscience, removes every sense of unworthiness and gives the right of access. The Spirit removes every sense of unfitness and breathes in burning love for God's honour and for souls.

**The call.** God and the world both need priests who live in his presence and carry the perishing on their hearts. Don't be content with being saved and doing just enough to stay warm. Be a priest, only a priest, and wholly a priest.
