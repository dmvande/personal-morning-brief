Title: "An holy priesthood," or, The Ministry of Intercession (1 Peter 2:5; Isaiah 61:6)

Every redeemed person shares Christ's priestly anointing, as the oil on Aaron's head ran down to the hem of his robe. Few use this privilege, yet it is our closest likeness to Jesus, who ever lives to pray.

**The priest's work.** It faces two ways. Toward God, the priest draws near, dwells in his presence and offers sacrifice and incense. Toward people, he carries their sins and needs to God and comes out to bless them in his name. A priest does not live for himself. Every believer is called to be God's priest among the perishing.

**The priest's walk.** Aaron's sons wore holy garments, avoided defilement and received no land, because God was their inheritance. Our power with God likewise depends on our life: separated from the world, even from some lawful things, wholly consecrated, and having God alone as our portion.

**The priest's consecration.** Aaron's sons were priests by birth but still needed consecration. They were washed, clothed, anointed with oil, and touched with blood on ear, hand and foot. Believers are priests by new birth, but they exercise the office by entering fully into the blood and the Spirit. The blood removes every sense of unworthiness and gives the right of access. The Spirit removes every sense of unfitness and gives burning love and power to intercede.

**The call.** God and the world both need priests. Don't be content with being saved and doing just enough to stay warm. Be a priest, only a priest, and wholly a priest.
