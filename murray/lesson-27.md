Title: "Father, I will," or, Christ the High Priest (John 17:24)

After giving unlimited promises about prayer, Jesus prays aloud. His high-priestly prayer shows what his intercession in heaven is like, and how we, as priests with him, are to intercede.

**He prays for himself.** "Father, glorify thy Son, that thy Son may glorify thee." *Our lesson:* come before the Father in Christ. Plead his finished work, give ourselves to God's glory, and ask that the Son be glorified in us. Only when our own relationship with God is clear can we intercede well for others.

**He prays for his own circle.** These are the disciples who received his word and are being sent into the world. He asks that they be kept from the evil one and sanctified by the truth. *Our lesson:* each of us has a circle. Pray specifically, give them the word as well as praying for them, and when they fall, pray instead of giving up: "Keep them; sanctify them."

**He prays for all believers.** He asks that they be one as a witness to the world and that they be with him in glory. *Our lesson:* pray for the whole church's unity in spirit and truth, not in outward organization.

**The manner: "Father, I will."** Jesus claimed the Father's promise, "Ask of me." Abiding in him, we may do the same. This is possible only for a will wholly surrendered to his, but to such a will the boldness is given.

Throw off the sloth that passes for humility, and take up the calling to plead and prevail for others.
